Then i made some other circle and some plain ovals to make a fish, i combined those too with the shape and combine tool and then proceeded to make two smaller plain ovals to make an X, i combined those too and placed on the fish as an eye. I made this selections with the shapes to symbolize pollution, bottle for the waste on the ocean of them, bubbles to represent the toxicity of it and the fish with an X representing death and finally the color blue to represent the ocean.
