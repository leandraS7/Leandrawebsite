Homework 6: vector eye

First the size is set on 1920 x 1080 points, kept in RGB and the DPI on 300. After i selected the paint brush on the Art section (Adobe illustrator on ipad). I used ink paint and fountain pen to draw the eye, tear drops- smoky tears and the little star. In a nutshell all of it; as well another pen called hand drawn for some texture around the tear-smoke. To color I selected a basic round brush on the blob brush section. For some other texture in the tear-smoke side, I used the floral brush (paint brush; art). For the background in selected a type of navy blue color; in between of light and dark. For the eyebrows the color is a brown hue and the brush is scroll pen 1. For the top eyelid and a little of the side was colored with a blob round brush. I used a calligraphy flat edge 40pt brush and lowered the size to 9 maybe , i wrote on the bottom of the eye ADONAI ORI, with the same brush but size 1 and signed my artist name on the tear of the eye. The emotion i attempted to express on this is awe and tearful gladness; a type of joy. Represented by the blue background , blue symbolizing tears, while the eye is looking upwards and the reflection of the awe its in it formed by the star on the center iris, the tears being smoked away are the gladness and joy being felt. Portraying overwhelming sensation not to be mistake it with sadness as the eye brow is up not in a sad expression. I Made other eyes in an illustration by 1920 x 1080, 5 eyes total depicting different emotions, from happy, irritated, suspicious, sad, and disappointed. I was going to add text to the eyes, naming each emotion but decided not to and let that to be decided by you since it’s more fun that way. This eyes have green because i think it’s a good contrast with the blue background. The center eye has different hues of green to depict its emotion, and the center iris is a red and because of the dark green it looks black, is the same colors as the center iris of the other eyes, its the color from the red section of the color wheel. The shapes i used were triangles and circles, i combined the triangles with the combine and shape builder tools and then i transformed it to make it oval looking to top to bottom and keep the pointy parts on the side.
