Homework 7: Illustrated Environment

House
Illustration

I downloaded an image of an environment on an old singular home. The environment is the woods, and the home seems to be solitary, just what I was looking for. The image i downloaded is from pexels.com, I searched for an old home in autumn. In Illustrator, I set the canvas to 1920 X 1080. I set the image on the canvas and lowered the opacity, then I used the shape tool to form the house, I used the square and triangle as well as the line tool. I modified the shape according to the home guideline with the direct selection tool. The lines of the house across the windows and door i used the dots brush. The shape tool i used it for shaping the lines of the trees, making bigger and smaller tree trunks. I used a line flat blob brush to make the branches. For the leafs, I used the start shape and used the repeat, grid, and radial tools to multiply it and grouped them to make the leafs more realistic. My used 3 colors: an earthy dull green, a dark navy blue, and violet. I used those colors for the shaping of the house, coloring, and the leaves. I chose this environment because it is a calm and peaceful environment just like the cold chills of autumn.
