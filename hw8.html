Homework 8: 2 Animated Gifs

First I created one duplicate of the Eye2 image and fixed each individual eye to be half blinking or half closed, than i made another duplicate of that and fully made the eyes close of course i also made the tears move with the sad eye. After that i exported each art board or image to photoshop this way. First open file > script > loaded into stacks and chose the 3 images of the blinking motion, then I went to windows opened the timeline panel and  selected create frame animation, clicked the bottom and went to the menu panel on the right and selected make frames from layers. I placed the animation on forever and selected the duration for each eye, opened is 0.1, half closed is 0.15 and closed is 0.2.  To export the gif i selected file > export > save for web and than made the gif size into 504 x 360 px , the colors 256 and the dither 100% and saved on my device as eyegif.
