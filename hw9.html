Homework 9: Data Visualization

Graph

First, I collected my data from my classmates and someone else in a Google Form of Video Game Preference. After I got my data, I set the canvas to 1920x1080, then I went to the 3 dots on the side of Illustrator on the toolbar and scrolled down to the graph tool. I started with the pie graph tool for my first 4 graphs. I shaped the squares in different sizes to place the pies, input my data in the Graph Data box, and checked the check mark. For the last graph, I used the column graph tool and placed the graph, input the data, and checked the mark. I turned on the grid and guide to align the canvas. The font I used was Times New Roman in bold. I then changed the color of some parts of the graphs by selecting the anchor points with the selection tool. On the last graph at the bottom, I left some colors unchanged to show other choices that weren’t selected.
